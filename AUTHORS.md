# Contributors

The software was developed by Alex Sixie Cao, with Asim Kumalic contributing
to code development during his master's thesis under Alex's supervision.
Alex was employed at ETH Zurich and Empa during much of the development;
software work also took place at Nanyang Technological University. The concept
originated in discussions between Alex and Sebastian Thöns during a research
stay at Lund University.

ETH Zurich is the copyright holder. Alex Sixie Cao and Asim Kumalic are credited
as the software creators; the associated paper lists all three paper authors.

The associated manuscript records the following contributions:

- **Alex Sixie Cao:** Conceptualization, Methodology, Software, Validation,
  Formal Analysis, Investigation, Data Curation, Writing – Original Draft,
  Writing – Review & Editing, Visualization, Supervision.
- **Asim Kumalic:** Methodology, Software.
- **Sebastian Thöns:** Conceptualization, Writing – Review & Editing,
  Funding Acquisition.

Software authorship in `CITATION.cff` identifies the manuscript's Software
contributors. The preferred paper citation lists all three manuscript authors.
